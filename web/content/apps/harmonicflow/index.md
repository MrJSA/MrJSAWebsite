## What it is

HarmonicFlow is the app I wanted on stage myself: one place for the songs, the setlist and everything the band
needs while playing. Songs are stored in the open **ChordPro** format, so they stay yours and work in other apps
too. Everything you need on stage is free — songs, setlists, the live view, transposing, metronome, tuner and an
ambient pad, with no ads in the performance screens.

More about the app, the privacy policy and a ChordPro editor for the browser are on
[harmonicflow.mrjsa.com](https://harmonicflow.mrjsa.com).

## Songs

- **ChordPro songs** — import, write and edit chord sheets in the open ChordPro format, from your phone or a cloud
  service. The editor shows a live preview while you type.
- **Song library** — search all your songs, and from one menu edit a song, duplicate it as another version (a radio
  edit, an acoustic arrangement …) or add it to a setlist.
- **Transpose & capo** — change the key in one tap, with the chords spelled correctly for the target key (B♭, not
  A♯), and play it with a capo. Key changes inside a song are followed, too.
- **Chord numbers** — show chords as Nashville numbers or Roman numerals, so the band can play in any key.
- **Musician notes** — notes for the whole band or just for one instrument, right where they belong in the song.
- **SongSelect & ChurchTools** — import songs from CCLI SongSelect for free; with Premium, take the song order of a
  service from ChurchTools as a setlist.

## On stage

- **Setlists** — plan the songs for a service or a gig, with the key and notes for each song. The app opens the
  setlist you used last.
- **Live view** — large, clean chord sheets for the stage. Swipe or use a page-turner pedal to go to the next song;
  columns, font size and sharps or flats can be set per song.
- **Metronome & ambient pad** — a metronome and an ambient pad in the key of the song, right next to the chords.
  The Warm pad is free; Premium adds more sounds, including two with soft ocean waves and a breeze.
- **PDF export** — print or send songs and whole setlists as PDF, in the font size you like, with guitar chord
  diagrams after the title or at the end.

## Tools for musicians

- **Tuner & sound analyser** — tune your instrument and see which notes are sounding. The audio never leaves your
  device.
- **Guitar chords** — how to finger the chords most bands play, with up to three ways each: open position first,
  then the barre chords.
- **Key finder, instrument ranges and the most common chord progressions** — quick references for rehearsal.

## Premium: one subscription for the whole band

- **Cloud sync** — songs and setlists stay the same on all your devices.
- **Shared setlists** — share a setlist with your band by link or QR code. Everyone sees changes to the order,
  keys and band notes right away; capo, font size and other personal settings stay on each phone. One device plays
  the metronome and pad for the whole setlist.
- **Only the one who shares pays** — band members join for free and receive every update; members with Premium can
  contribute keys, notes and songs themselves.

## Made with care

- Available in **English, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese**, with a light and a dark theme.
- Works without an internet connection; sync runs in the background and never gets in the way on stage.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking. The sync server runs on my own server in the EU, and the audio of tuner
  and sound analyser never leaves your phone.
