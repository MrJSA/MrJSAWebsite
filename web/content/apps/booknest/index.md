## What it is

BookNest is my home library on the phone: scan a book's barcode, and title, authors and cover are filled in by
themselves. After that you always know where each book is — on which shelf, lent to whom, or borrowed from whom
and until when.

The library lives on your phone and works without internet or an account. It is free, with no ads.

More about the app, the privacy policy and the terms are on
[booknest.mrjsa.com](https://booknest.mrjsa.com).

## Your books

- **Scan the ISBN** — point the camera at the barcode and the book is in, or type the ISBN. No camera permission
  needed: the scanning runs on the phone through Google's code scanner.
- **Details by themselves** — title, authors, cover and more from Google Books, the German National Library,
  K10plus, lobid.org and Open Library. Books still missing a cover or details are looked up again later and filled
  in.
- **Your library** — search, filter and sort by title, author, your rating or return date, and note which shelf
  each book is on.
- **Every copy** — hardcover, paperback or pocket book. BookNest asks before you add a second copy of a book.
- **Ratings and favourites** — half stars, a heart for your favourites and a mark for the books you have read.

## Lent, borrowed and given away

- **Who has which book** — record who you lent a book to or borrowed it from, since when and until when. Overdue
  books stand out on the home screen.
- **Where is it?** — each book's page shows where it is, with the actions that fit: lend it out, give it away, got
  it back, gave it back.
- **Nothing gets lost** — books you gave away or borrowed books you gave back are remembered, so you know their
  story.
- **Wishlist** — books you would like, kept apart from your library. When you get one, "Got it" moves it onto your
  shelf.

## Premium: the whole family's books in one place

- **Cloud sync** — your library stays the same on all your phones and tablets.
- **Family library** — everyone keeps their own library and sees the family's books in it too, with whose they
  are. Anyone can lend a family book out or note where it is kept; only its owner changes its details.
- **Personal where it matters** — ratings, read marks and notes stay your own; a book shows the family's average
  rating.
- **Family Premium** — for you and up to 5 more people, who get Premium through yours for free as long as it runs.

## Made with care

- In **English and German**, with a light and a dark theme that follow your phone.
- Works offline first; no account is needed until you want to sync. Sign in with Google or an e-mail address.
- No advertising, analytics or tracking. Your books are only sent to the sync server when you sync, and it runs on
  my own server in the EU.
